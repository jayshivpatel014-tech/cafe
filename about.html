Our story

About Butter & Bloom

Home / About
"

It started with one birthday cake, made for a neighbor who couldn't find a bakery open on a Sunday.

That single cake in 2013 turned into a weekend hobby, then a waiting list, then a tiny storefront on Bakers Lane. Twelve years later, Butter & Bloom is still run by the same hands that mixed that first batch of batter — we've just added a few more ovens.

We bake in small batches, use real butter and seasonal fruit, and never pre-mix more than a day's worth of frosting. It's slower, but it's the only way we know how to make a cake taste like someone actually cared.
